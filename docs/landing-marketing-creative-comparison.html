Riffing on A: roomier calendar control tower.

The calendar-first concept is working, but the fake week view was too tight. These three versions keep A's credibility and fix the cramped product surface in different ways: a wider week, a focused-day agenda, and a route-board calendar.

  • Can family balance, shorter chores, Google Calendar, and properties all fit?
