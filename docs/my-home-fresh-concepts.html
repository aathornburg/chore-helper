Fresh My Home concepts

Forget the old page. What should setting up a home feel like?

These concepts start from the user journey: help someone model a household quickly, understand what Cleanly knows, and make edits without turning the page into a form cave.

Concept A

Home setup studio

Recommended

A playful, build-the-model experience. The house is large, dimensional, and always present. The right side guides the user through floors, rooms, and surfaces without making every action look primary.

Room map canvas

Why it feels good

The page has a clear next step, so setup feels lighter for new users. Existing users can still jump into Floors or Rooms from the model.

Most fun and balanced

Concept A gives the house the strongest identity while keeping the app useful for repeated editing.

Most tactile

Concept B is excellent if room setup is the heart of the page, but it would require the biggest interaction shift.

Best for onboarding

Concept C makes household setup feel easy, though it is less powerful as a long-term management view.
