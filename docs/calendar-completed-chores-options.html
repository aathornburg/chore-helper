Calendar UX exploration

Completed chores should be visible, but visually quiet.

I agree with your starting point. Completed chores should not compete with to-be-done chores because the primary job of the calendar is deciding what still needs attention. My recommendation is Option A: a small Completed group inside each day, with plain text-style clickable rows.

Option A

Completed group under planned chores

Completed chores move into a compact text section at the bottom of the day. They are still clickable, but lose the “bubble” treatment. This keeps completion visible while protecting space and attention for remaining work.

Soft divider with checkmarked rows

This is slightly more compact than Option A and feels more like an audit trail. The checkmark makes completion status obvious, but the section label is less explicit.
