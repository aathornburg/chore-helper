Calendar density direction

Less detail in the grid, more context on click

The calendar should be scannable first. Chore details, completion, upcoming occurrences, and history should live in a read-only view modal opened by clicking the chore.

Direction Options

A

Recommended: title-only calendar cards

Month and week show only chore titles. Day view can show title, time, duration, and assignee, but no redundant flexible chip.

Best fit for the density problem.

B

Title plus tiny metadata

Month stays title-only, but week keeps duration or assignee text. This is more informative, but still risks crowding.

C

Side panel instead of modal

Clicking a chore opens a side panel. Good for browsing, but it costs horizontal space and complicates responsive behavior.
