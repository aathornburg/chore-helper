Owner queue UX comparison

Make calendar imports feel reviewable, not spreadsheet-y.

These options use the same pending events but change how the owner reviews them. The goal is to support batches of 30+ imports without wrapping columns, oversized empty panels, or too many primary-feeling buttons.

A. Queue + inspector B. Row drawers C. Approval cards D. Grouped by day

Option A

Compact queue with a selected-item inspector

This is the direction I was about to implement, but tightened up: fewer columns, no full-height empty right pane, and a detail panel that actually earns its space. Best when the owner wants to arrow through many items quickly.

Option B

Each event opens as a drawer

This keeps context in one column and avoids the awkward right pane entirely. It is my current favorite for Cleanly: owners scan the queue, expand only what they need, and act directly below the item.

Option D

Grouped by day, built for calendar thinking

This aligns nicely with the Calendar page: imports are reviewed in the same mental model as where they will land. It is less compact than drawers, but better when timing conflicts and calendar context matter.
