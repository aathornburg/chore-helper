Import modal range trigger

Make the date range feel like a control, not a drawer handle.

These options replace the separate Change range link with a clickable date range. The goal is to keep the modal event-first while making it obvious that Jun 1-Jun 30 is the thing you can edit.

A. Date pill popover B. Inline text popover C. Sleek inline drawer

Option A

Date pill opens a compact popover

Best balance. The date looks clickable without adding another command. The popover keeps range controls out of the event flow, and it can close after preset selection or stay open for custom dates.

Quietest version. It treats the range like editable metadata, which keeps the strip light. The risk is that some users may not realize the date is clickable unless hover/focus states are strong.
